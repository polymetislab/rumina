Rumina

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Rumina is made by Polymetislab.

  • No account. You never sign up or log in to Rumina.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Rumina contains no third-party code.
  • Your log stays yours. Feeds live on your iPhone and, if you share them, in your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted. Nothing about your baby reaches us.

What Rumina keeps, and where

On your iPhoneFeeds, babies' names, and settings. Protected by your iPhone's passcode and built-in encryption. Optionally behind Face ID (Settings → Privacy).
In your iCloud, only if you share the logAn end-to-end encrypted copy in your private iCloud, shared only with the people you invite. Apple stores it under its iCloud terms. Neither Apple nor Polymetislab can read the feeds or names; Apple can see only technical details such as when records change and how much storage they use.
Backups you exportA file saved wherever you choose (for example, Files or iCloud Drive). It contains your log in readable form, so keep it private.

Apple services Rumina uses

  • iCloud for the shared log (see above).
  • Apple Weather, only if you turn on Settings → Weather. Your approximate location (about city level) is sent to Apple to get today's high and low. Rumina keeps only that forecast, never your location.
  • Siri, if you use voice commands. Speech is processed by Apple under its Siri terms.
  • Face ID, if you turn on the lock. Handled entirely by iOS; Rumina never receives biometric data.
  • App Store purchases (Rumina Plus). Payment is handled by Apple. We receive only Apple's aggregate sales reports, not who you are.
  • Crash reports, only if you turned on "Share With App Developers" in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Apple may then share anonymous crash reports with us.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or share your information.
  • Use your information for advertising, or track you across apps and websites.
  • Run servers that store your family's data.

Sharing with your doctor

"Send to my doctor" creates a PDF on your iPhone and opens the iPhone share sheet. Nothing is sent until you choose an app and a recipient.

Children

Rumina is for parents and caregivers, not for children. The log describes a baby and is entered by the parent; we don't collect it.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Erase all data removes the log from this iPhone and your part of the shared log in iCloud.
  • Stop sharing or leave: Settings → Shared log.
  • Weather: turn it off in Settings → Weather, or remove location access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • iCloud storage: manage it in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and mention it in the app's release notes.
